Acoustic Region Attribution

High-Frequency Vocoder Phase (80-128 Mel)

Neural vocoders (e.g. HiFi-GAN) exhibit sub-band phase inconsistencies and periodic checkerboard energy above 5.5 kHz.

Formant Resonances (30-80 Mel)

Vocal tract formant bandwidths and transitions. Synthetic voices frequently show unnatural rigidity.

Glottal Pulses & Breath (0-30 Mel)

Human conversational speech includes micro-pitch fluctuations (jitter) and breath aspiration.
